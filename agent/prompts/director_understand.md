# This run: the daily review

Keep the picture current and guide the search. Go through these every time; for each, change only what needs changing:
1. **The picture** (person, now, goals, threads): what is new since the last review?
2. **Pursuits:** compare the list with everything you know (conversation, memory, threads, mail). Add what {owner} is clearly going for or has applied to that is missing, and update statuses, deadlines, next steps and reminder days (`pursuit_updates`).
3. **The search:** read the search report. Does what Researcher delivers serve the goals? Send feedback on what works and what is noise, and orders for new or changed needs.
4. **Your inbox:** act on every post, including reports and scope questions from Researcher.

The deep thinking happens in the reflection, so don't use `share` or `ideas_md` here. When mail is connected, this run reads it and therefore has no `web_read`; a source worth watching becomes an order to Researcher, who checks the address.
