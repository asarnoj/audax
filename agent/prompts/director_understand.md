# This run: the daily review

Keep the picture and the plans current, and guide the search. Go through these; for each, change only what needs changing:
1. **The review below:** act on what is due, stalled or waiting (your cleaning pass), and on the answers to your questions.
2. **The picture:** what is new since the last review? Update beliefs (add `observations`: dated lines of what was seen), the sections of `now.md` that changed, and `person.md` if the story changed.
3. **What {owner} goes for, and the plans:** compare the aims with what you know (conversation, memory, mail). Keep them true, and keep each active goal's plan alive in steps with owners and dates.
4. **The search:** does what Researcher delivers serve the goals? Feedback on what works and what is noise; orders or steps for new needs.
5. **Your inbox:** act on every post.
6. **Triage, your fast skill:** when the review has a sample of what it did (Triagens stickprov), check it against what you know of {owner} now, and answer `triage_review`. A drop that was wrong goes in `wrong` and comes back to the desk; the cure is a clearer line in `now.md` or the goals, where triage reads, not a rule for one case.

Deep, free thinking belongs to the reflection; a thought for {owner} (`thoughts`) that today's material gives, a small experiment or a question is fine here too. When mail is connected, this run reads it and has no `web_read`; a source worth watching becomes an order to Researcher.
